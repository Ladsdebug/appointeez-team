Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

You pay per lead. Pricing depends on your service area and monthly volume. We'll quote it on the call after showing you a sample.

Where do the leads come from?

We run our own marketing to homeowners looking for artificial turf installation, then phone-verify each one before it reaches you.

Are the leads exclusive to me?

Yes. Each lead is sold once, to one installer, in one service area. We never resell it.

How are leads qualified?

Every lead is phone-verified. We confirm the homeowner has an active artificial turf installation need and is inside your service area before sending it.

What if a lead is bad?

We replace a lead only if the phone number is dead - disconnected or not in service. We don't replace leads for any other reason.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No. Start with a small test batch in your area before deciding on monthly volume.
