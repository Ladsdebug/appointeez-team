Frequently asked questions

How are leads qualified before I get them?

Every lead is phone-verified: we confirm the homeowner has an active HVAC repair or install need and that it falls inside your chosen service area before it's sent to you.

Are the leads exclusive to me?

Yes. Each lead is sold once, to one contractor, in one service area. We don't resell it to anyone else.

What happens if a lead is bad?

If a lead doesn't meet the criteria we promised, it's swapped for a new one at no extra cost.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No. Start with a small test batch in your service area before committing to a monthly volume.
